Who and what made this walk

Matisse's Montparnasse · Paris, 21 October 2026

Andrey Sadovykh

Original idea and concept, product design and direction, content validation, guide of the walk. Every decision about what the walk is, what it says and how it looks; every fact read against the sources before it went in.

Research, texts, code and briefs

Written with Claude (Anthropic) under Andrey's direction: the historical research with its sources, the station texts and captions, the episode and drawing briefs, the website, the map rendering and the booklet. Texts CC BY 4.0.

Voices

The eight audio episodes were generated with Google NotebookLM from our fact-checked notes and transcribed and checked line by line. The notes are the reference, not the recordings.

Drawings

The eight comic pages were drawn with Google Gemini in a paper-cut style from our panel-by-panel scripts; the captions are ours. No real person is depicted.

Map

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL); vector tiles by OpenFreeMap; rendered with MapLibre GL JS. Routes measured on OpenStreetMap's pedestrian network. Poster maps drawn from the same data.

Sources

Museum and archive records (Centre Pompidou, SFMOMA, MoMA, Musée Rodin, Hermitage, Van Gogh Museum, Musée Matisse, Bibliothèque nationale de France, Paris Musées), Hilary Spurling's biography of Matisse, the Société d'histoire du 7e arrondissement, Wikipedia in several languages as a last resort, and the opening hours of the Sénat for the Luxembourg garden. Anything we could not source was left out or marked "the story goes".

Type

Fraunces, Karla and Patrick Hand, from Google Fonts.

Pictures

All artworks are in the public domain in France and the EU (artists dead more than 70 years; Matisse since 1 January 2025). Photographs under a Creative Commons licence carry their photographer's name. Every image on the site and in the booklet is listed here with its licence.
